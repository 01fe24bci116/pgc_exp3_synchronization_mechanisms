Expected correct counter: 10000000

Analysis:
  - 'None' produces non-deterministic loss: 6,088,479 at 2 threads and degrades to
    3,407,513 at 8 threads (66% loss of updates).
  - 'Critical' achieves 100% correctness (10,000,000) but execution time rises
    from 0.1127s (1 thread) to 0.2852s (8 threads) due to heavy serialization.
  - 'Atomic' achieves 100% correctness (10,000,000) with significantly lower
    latency than Critical (0.0681s at 1 thread, 0.1760s at 8 threads).

Environmental Variations & Explanations:
  1. On Windows with MinGW libgomp, `omp_lock_t` relies on Windows CriticalSection/
     Event synchronization primitives, which incur significant user-to-kernel
     transition overhead under heavy lock contention (10,000,000 acquisitions).
  2. In contrast, on Linux (Ubuntu 22.04 with glibc / native futexes), `omp_lock_t`
     overhead is much closer to `#pragma omp critical`.
  3. 'Atomic' remains exceptionally efficient across all platforms because it
     bypasses OS-level mutex primitives and maps directly to atomic CPU instructions
     (LOCK CMPXCHG / LOCK XADD on x86-64).
